    Looks only, never power. Earn Goo by popping (more for Perfects and long streaks). In the iOS app, bundles can also be bought; everything stays earnable.

    How to play

    1. Tap to jump. Tap in the air to slam straight down. The marker under Dex shows where you'll land.
    2. Slam when the marker turns gold, right on a pimple's white head: PERFECT. Biggest launch, more speed, +1 streak. The dome is a Great, the rim is an OK.
    3. Every launch flies over several pimples: pick one. Skip red ones (not ripe). Yellow ones are overripe: a mega launch, but goo splatters your screen.
    4. A red ✕ means danger: spikes, hair or snapping tweezers below you.
    5. Hair ahead? There's always a yellow pimple before it: slam it to fly over. Missing the head tilts the launch: back edge = higher, front edge = faster.
    6. Come down on mosquitoes from above to bounce across gaps.
    7. Speed pushes the foam wall back. Misses slow you down. If the foam catches you, or a hazard does, the run is over.
    8. Cysts (on the back): land, hold, let go at full charge. Grab glowing bacteria for 10-second mutations.
    9. Daily Pop: the same course for everyone today. Race the ghost of your best run.

    Keyboard: Space / ↑ / W to tap (hold for cysts), P or Esc to pause.
